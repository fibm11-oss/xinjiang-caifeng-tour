    Private groups

    One vehicle. Only your people.

    No shared transfers, no fixed departure whistle. Vehicle, driver and daily pace are all built around your party.

    Why a private group

    Xinjiang is vast — transport shapes the whole trip

    At 1.66 million km², the distance between highlights is often 300–400 km. Public transport reaches the cities, but not the sunrise at Sayram Lake, the morning mist in Hemu, or an unplanned stop on a bend of the Duku Highway.

    A private group is, in our experience, the right way to travel here: 2 to 8 people form one party, with a dedicated vehicle and a local driver who knows the roads. Want an extra hour somewhere, or a different route? Just say so that morning.

    This works equally well for inbound travellers — we confirm English communication, airport transfers, dietary and religious requirements before departure, and a dedicated contact stays with you throughout.

    What is included

    Four things every trip includes

    Not tiered by package — these are standard on every journey.

    01

    Your own vehicle & local driver

    One vehicle per party, never shared. Drivers are experienced Xinjiang locals who know long-distance conditions, seasonal closures and local languages.

    02

    Optional private guide

    For deeper context on Uyghur culture, Silk Road history and regional food, add a guide who travels with you, interprets and helps with on-the-ground issues.

    03

    Full insurance & licensed operation

    Vehicles carry liability and motor insurance, drivers hold valid licences, and maintenance follows local tourism-vehicle regulations.

    04

    A group chat from day one

    A private chat opens on departure day for route changes, road conditions, meals and lodging — answers in real time, not when you get back to the hotel.
