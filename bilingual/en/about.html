    About Curated Xinjiang

    Travel Xinjiang with people who actually live here

    Not a fixed package, but a journey written around you — with someone looking after every detail along the way. Here is how we work, and what you may want to know first.

    Why travel with us

    We handle the details. You keep the views.

    Three things we will never do, and three we always will.

    01

    A local driver and a private guide

    One vehicle dedicated to your party. The driver knows the road conditions; the guide knows the stories behind them — and where to stop for the better photograph.

    02

    A group chat from day one

    We open a dedicated chat the moment your trip starts. Route changes, weather and small requests are answered in real time, not at the end of the day.

    03

    No rushing, no shopping stops

    No compulsory early starts and no commission shops. An extra hour by the lake, or turning back for better light, is exactly what a private trip is for.

    How it works

    Three steps to your own itinerary

    You are not choosing a package. You are telling us what you want, and we write the plan.

    01

    Tell us your style

    Northern landscapes, southern Silk Road culture, photography, family travel, a quiet trip for two — start with whatever you have in mind.

    02

    Give us dates & group size

    How many days, how many people, and whether you prefer a full schedule or long unhurried stops. We shape the route around that.

    03

    We write the roadbook

    Using that season's bloom, daylight and road conditions, we send back a day-by-day plan with stops, stays and transfer times.
