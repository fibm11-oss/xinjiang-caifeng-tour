Why a private group

Xinjiang is vast — transport shapes the whole trip

At 1.66 million km², the distance between highlights is often 300–400 km. Public transport reaches the cities, but not the sunrise at Sayram Lake, the morning mist in Hemu, or an unplanned stop on a bend of the Duku Highway.

A private group is, in our experience, the right way to travel here: 2 to 8 people form one party, with a dedicated vehicle and a local driver who knows the roads. Want an extra hour somewhere, or a different route? Just say so that morning.

This works equally well for inbound travellers — we confirm English communication, airport transfers, dietary and religious requirements before departure, and a dedicated contact stays with you throughout.

Pricing

How pricing works

A private group is priced around a daily vehicle charter, not a per-person package. Four things drive the quote: number of days, vehicle type, season, and optional guide or special transfers.

Usually included

  • Dedicated vehicle and driver, fuel and motorway tolls
  • Driver meals, lodging and long-distance allowance
  • Tour liability and vehicle insurance
  • Itinerary design, lodging recommendations and pre-departure consulting

Usually separate

  • Accommodation and meals (we can book; charged at the level you choose)
  • Park tickets, shuttle buses, horse riding and performances
  • Private guide add-on, international and domestic flights
  • Personal shopping, visas and travel insurance
Why no price list? Vehicle and hotel rates can vary several-fold between low and high season, and every party has a different route and headcount. Send us your dates, party size and wish list and we will reply with an itemised quote — not a vague "from" figure.

International travellers may be quoted in CNY, USD or TWD; payment method and deposit terms are confirmed together with the itinerary.
