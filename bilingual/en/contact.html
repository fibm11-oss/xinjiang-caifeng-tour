    FAQ

    Quick answers

    Q: How far ahead should I book?

    For peak season (Jun–Sep) we suggest 15–30 days, and 7–10 days in the quieter months. If your dates are close, do still ask — we will check vehicle and room availability and do our best to make it work.

    Q: Do you charge a planning or booking fee?

    No. Consultation and itinerary planning are free, and we do not add a service charge when booking accommodation, tickets or vehicles on your behalf. We also look for package rates where they help.

    Q: When is the Duku Highway open?

    Usually early June to early October, depending on that year's weather and snowmelt. We share the latest road status in advance and keep an alternative routing ready so your itinerary never stalls.
