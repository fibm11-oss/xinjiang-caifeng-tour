City walk

Slow down by the river before heading into the mountains

Burqin is small enough for one night — and just large enough to stay with you for the whole trip.

The route at a glance

Designed for an evening arrival. After a long drive, three hours of riverbank and night market beats one more scheduled sight.

  • 17:00–18:30 Burqin Bridge & the Irtysh River
  • 18:30–19:30 Russian-style street
  • 19:30–22:00 Riverside night market

Stop 1: Burqin Bridge & the Irtysh (17:00–18:30)

The Irtysh is China's only river system flowing to the Arctic Ocean. Late light falls across the water and birch woods, and the temperature finally drops. It is windy on the bridge — bring a layer.

  • The riverside path is flat and easy for children and older travellers
  • Best light is the half hour around sunset
  • The coloured cliffs of Wucaitan need a separate transfer — do not squeeze them into this walk

Stop 2: Russian-style street (18:30–19:30)

Geography and history left this small town a set of painted wooden houses and Russian-style window trim. The quarter takes half an hour to cross; the point is the border-town quiet as the lights come on.

Stop 3: Riverside night market (19:30–22:00)

The main event is grilled fish. Cold-water fish from the Irtysh is slowly charred over coals with cumin and chilli; add a glass of kvass and you have the definitive Burqin meal.

  • Eat: grilled pike, lamb skewers, spicy chicken, kvass
  • Fish is priced by size — confirm weight and price before ordering
  • In summer the market only gets going after dark; too early and stalls are still closed
Burqin is a necessary stop on northern routes. Driving north from Ürümqi on the S21 desert highway, the first day usually ends here; the next day into Kanas and Hemu is then much shorter.

Practical information

  • Lodging: confirm early in peak season (July–August and autumn colour) — a small town has limited rooms
  • Climate: the riverbank is windy and cool at dusk, even in summer
  • Getting around: the town is walkable; Wucaitan and Hemu require a vehicle
  • Payment: mobile payment is common at the market, but carry some cash

One night in Burqin makes the mountain days far more relaxed. That is why it stays in both our 16- and 17-day itineraries.
