    At a glance

    What this version adds

    The routing resembles the 17-day loop, but lingers longer between Ili and the Duku: the Yizhao Highway, Tekes and a full day at Nalati are there so you stay somewhere rather than merely pass through.

    The other difference is the audio guide. We pre-recorded roughly one-minute Chinese commentaries for 25 stops along the route. Play one just before arrival — no geolocation, no background polling, no data chasing you around.

    The clips are pre-recorded human narration, played on click and embedded in the itinerary rather than a separate app. The English edition translates the interface and titles; narration remains in Chinese, with English recording planned later.

    Good to know

    Flights, connections and pitfalls

    Flights and transfers

    The sample uses two connecting flights each way. Outbound, collect baggage and stay near the transit airport; the next day a dedicated driver meets you in Ürümqi. Homebound, Turpan returns to the airport the same day, so departure must be before 13:00.

    Daily distances

    The longest are day 3 on the desert highway (approx. 530–600 km / 6–7.5 hrs) and day 7 (approx. 538 km / 6.5 hrs). Most others fall between 150 and 260 km, and long days are followed by walking days inside scenic areas.

    Key cautions

    • Baggage must be rechecked at the transit airport — allow at least three hours between flights
    • The Yizhao and Duku highways are seasonal; if closed we reroute via Tekes and the river valleys
    • Turpan can exceed 40°C in summer — midday is for interiors or a rest
    • Mountain days swing sharply in temperature; keep sun protection, warmth and water in the vehicle

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need an app or location services?

    No. It is a player on the page: click to play, with no geolocation, no tracking and no polling — just static files.

    Is there an English narration?

    All 25 clips are currently in Chinese. The English page translates titles and notes and labels the language; English recording is on the roadmap.

    How do I choose between 16 and 17 days?

    Pick 17 if you want Turpan and more city time; pick 16 if you would rather go deeper into Ili, the Yizhao Highway and the audio commentaries.

    Can I take just one section?

    Yes. The five-day "Sayram Lake – Yining – Yizhao – Nalati" stretch is the most commonly extracted and attaches well to other plans.
