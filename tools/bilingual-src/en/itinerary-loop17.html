17 days · Grand northern loop

See the whole north, once and properly

From desert highway to snow peaks and alpine lakes, from Ili grasslands to an oasis city. Seventeen days stay unhurried because three full days have no driving at all.

At a glance

Why seventeen days

Distance eats time in Xinjiang. To hold Altay, Ili, the Duku and Turpan in a single journey without watching the scenery through a car window, 17 days is the comfortable minimum we have measured on the ground.

Three full days involve no transfer: Hemu, Nalati and Turpan. Every long driving day is followed by a slow one, so both you and your camera get to catch up.

Best from June to October. Late September to early October brings the northern autumn colour; the Duku and Yizhao highways are only included during their open season.

Good to know

Flights, distances and practical notes

Flight connections

The sample uses two connecting flights in and out of Ürümqi, with a full buffer day on each side so an international delay cannot compress the trip. Transit cities and flight numbers change annually and are rechecked for your origin.

Daily distances

The longest is day 7, Urho to Sayram Lake at roughly 538 km / 6.5 hrs; day 3 across the desert highway runs 530–600 km. Most other days sit between 150 and 280 km, and long days are paired with slow ones.

Notes and cautions

  • The Duku and Yizhao highways are seasonal; snowmelt, rockfall or controls trigger a reroute after checking the current year's notice
  • Park tickets, shuttle buses, riding and shows are normally separate and itemised in the quote
  • Mountain areas swing sharply between day and night temperatures — pack warmth and sun protection even in summer
  • For border or restricted areas requiring permits, we confirm the process with you in advance
Customisation: seventeen days is the fullest version, not the right one for everybody. With less leave we can drop the two Turpan days or trade the southern leg for a deeper city stay — both are simple edits.

FAQ

Common questions

How many long driving days are there?

About four exceed five hours; the rest are 2–4 hours or mostly walking. Long days never stack consecutively.

Can we arrive via southern Xinjiang and depart from the north?

Yes, but the routing has to be rebuilt. Give us your arrival city and we recalculate distances and overnight stops.

When is autumn colour most reliable?

Usually late September to early October in the north, shifting by about a week with the year's temperatures. We report current foliage conditions before departure.

Can dietary and religious needs be handled?

Yes. Halal, vegetarian, allergies and prayer times are confirmed in advance and built into restaurant and rest stops.

Want seventeen days at your pace?

Adding or cutting days, or changing season, can all start from this framework.
