City walk

The "Shanghai of the Gobi" — the last stop before the mountains

A city born of reclamation and beautified by greenery, also the refuel-and-restart point before the Duku Highway.

Kuitun, seat of the 7th Division of the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps, is a city born of reclamation and beautified by greenery, called by many the "Shanghai of the Gobi". For those driving or chartering the Duku Highway, Kuitun (adjacent to Dushanzi) is often the last proper city before the mountains — fuel, supplies and rest all start here. It is also Northern Xinjiang's crossroads: national highways 217, 312 and 218 meet, the G30 Lianhuo expressway and North Xinjiang railway run through, west to the four ports of Khorgas, Alashankou, Baktu and Jeminay; east to Urumqi, Changji and Shihezi, west to Dushanzi and Wusu. Shawan — the famed birthplace of Big Plate Chicken — sits just east.

The route at a glance

  • Morning: urban artificial-river greenbelt
  • Lunch: Big Plate Chicken Street
  • Afternoon: Dushanzi Grand Canyon (before Duku)
  • En route: Red Mountain Canyon / Kuitun River Canyon

Kuitun's own City Walk is easy: the city is green, the artificial-river belt links parks and squares; but what truly draws photographers is the nearby Kuitun River Canyon (a.k.a. Red Mountain Canyon) and the Dushanzi Grand Canyon at the Duku Highway's kilometre-zero. This route gathers "city rest" and "canyon shots" in one go.

Urban artificial-river greenbelt (morning)

Half of Kuitun's "Shanghai of the Gobi" name comes from its surprising green — the Runmin River and artificial-river belt link parks, squares and promenades, deep shade in summer. A morning riverside stroll watches how a reclamation new town grew from wasteland into a garden city — the most relaxed opening of the walk. The city is small; shared bikes or walking are both comfortable.

Big Plate Chicken Street (lunch)

You can't talk Kuitun without Big Plate Chicken — whose home is Shawan County, east in Tacheng. In the 1980s, Sichuan chef Li Shilin ran the "Manpengge" eatery by Shawan's highway; his best-seller was "chilli-fried chicken". Early reform era saw road transport boom; long-haul drivers left Urumqi at dawn along G312 and reached Shawan right at lunch — the place became drivers' canteen. Someone then served the chilli chicken in an enamel platter, it blew up, and "a big plate of chilli chicken" became "Big Plate Chicken". Trademarked in 1992, Shawan's cooking craft joined Xinjiang's intangible heritage in 2024. Authentic ingredients are all local Shawan: Anjihai chilli skins, Wulanwusu scallions, Bo'ertonggu potatoes, Xigebi free-range chicken, with belt noodles — irresistible aroma. Eat it in Kuitun and taste this Xinjiang calling card.

Dushanzi Grand Canyon (afternoon, before Duku)

Beside G217 in Dushanzi District, Karamay, it is the first sight at the Duku Highway's kilometre-zero. Glacial melt over hundreds of millions of years cut a near north-south "U" cleft: the Kuitun valley runs about 20 km, up to 200 m vertical, with at least nine clear terraces on the cliffs like tree rings recording geology — featured by National Geographic, and a filming site for "Pegasus" and "Novoland: Pearl Eclipse". The main viewing platform and plank path already awe; the glass bridge etc. are人造 net-red projects, not essential for the view. Park, no shuttle, 1.5–2 hours easy. Golden light is 18:00–20:00 when sunset slants on red rock — the travel-photo window.

Red Mountain Canyon / Kuitun River Canyon (en route)

Same Kuitun valley system, the Red Mountain Canyon (Kuitun River Canyon) is the red gorge cut by the Kuitun River, kin to Dushanzi, similar style — a worthwhile extra shot when time allows. For something wilder, the Anjihai Grand Canyon is wild, redder, a drone holy site, but with no rails, no path and high fall risk — must go in groups, away from cliffs; not for newbies with kids. Before returning, detour to the Dushanzi mud volcano (active crater) and the Duku Highway Museum (kilometre-zero stele at the door) to read "why Duku is worth it" at once.

Locals know: the Duku Highway links Dushanzi and Kuqa, 561 km across the Tianshan, open roughly early June to October, several seasons in one day — the northern kilometre-zero starts at Dushanzi. The northern section enforces "mandatory reservation": 5 time slots, releasing the 7th day's quota at 00:00 daily. The right rhythm is "canyons + city supply in the morning, then enter the north section by reserved slot" — don't miss your slot by playing too long and get turned back. For Big Plate Chicken, Shawan bloodline is most authentic; Kuitun is the supply stop, easy for food and lodging. Want canyon shots but fear the人造 items? Remember: the grand canyon already holds the real, hundred-million-year gorge; the rest is extra.

Restaurants & cafés to try

Kuitun is a Northern Xinjiang transit hub; dining's soul is "Big Plate Chicken" — Shawan bloodline most authentic, Wangfei and Jianzhai are brands locals grew up with. Cafés are fewer than big cities, but chains and independents both exist, a good cup on the way. The chicken serving is huge; two people usually share one.

Where locals eat

  • Wangfei Big Plate Chicken · Xinjiang Cuisine · BBQ (Kuitun): since 1999, on the 2025 must-eat list. Small-pot simmered, Anjihai chilli skins, the chicken plate is "really big", potatoes soft and floury, broth amazing with noodles; thin pancakes, old yoghurt, yellow-noodle BBQ all praised.
  • Jianzhai Big Plate Chicken (Wusu Rd): hand-grabbed lamb tender without gaminess, baked buns crisp; the chicken noodles coated in rich broth — and friendly on the wallet.
  • Yiganqi Pilaf / Yima Lane Snack Street: Yiganqi pilaf is oily yet not greasy, the top carb refill; for a sweep, Yima Lane, Hongqi snack street and Tuanjie night market gather baked buns, cold noodles, pepper-ma chicken — good for walking and eating.

Cafés & drinks

  • Zuo's Coffee: an old neighbourhood shop on Tuanjie Road, fragrant cappuccino, dense foam, the warm owner reminds you of portion sizes. Opens 07:30, sit in early or afternoon.
  • Cotti Coffee (Kuitun Wusu St): starry oat latte with pretty layers, photogenic, dark beans with oat milk not astringent — a cup on the run. Stable chain quality.
  • Independents around Zhongjin City Plaza: around the plaza on East Beijing Road, Zhuiming, Tutu, Wakaka and other independents gather, varied styles, friendly — where locals nest for an afternoon.

Practical information

  • Getting there: Kuitun is a Northern hub, ~3.5 hrs from Urumqi; walk/bike in town; Dushanzi Grand Canyon ~30 min self-drive from Dushanzi centre.
  • Best season: Jun–Oct (matches Duku's open season); summer canyons best at dusk, winter snow pretty but some roads controlled.
  • Suggested stay: half-day town + half-day canyon; mostly a Duku pre-transfer, an overnight to rest works.
  • Dress & sun: canyons windy, summer scorching — hat, sunglasses, SPF50+ and windproof jacket; non-slip shoes and hold children near cliffs.
  • Local notes: reserve the Duku northern slot ahead; glass bridge etc. by preference, don't be swayed by "you came all this way"; Anjihai wild canyon is extremely dangerous, not for newbies with kids; limited supplies in scenic areas, bring water and snacks.
